# Contributing to messaging

The Astro messaging bridge: a Go/gRPC service that connects agents to chat surfaces (Slack, web), plus client SDKs for Go, TypeScript, and Python.

For layout, setup, building, testing, and configuration, see the [README](README.md).

## Issues

The easiest way to contribute is to open an issue with a bug report or a suggestion for improvement. Please include enough detail for us to understand and reproduce the problem or evaluate the request.

## How to contribute

1. **Fork** the repository.
2. **Create a branch** for your changes.
3. **Open a pull request** describing what changed and why.

Opening a pull request signs off on your contribution under the [Developer Certificate of Origin](#developer-certificate-of-origin) and the terms below.

## Contributions of source code

All such contributions should be in the form of pull requests.
